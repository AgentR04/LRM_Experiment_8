Minor in Big Data Engineering Semester: VIII
Program: Common for All Programs (except Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science))
Course: Big Data Engineering (DJS22MN4C4)
Pre-requisite: Database Systems, SQL

Objectives: To introduce students to the fundamental concepts of Big data platforms and Hadoop
Ecosystem.

Outcomes: On completion of the course, the learner will be able to:
1. Apply appropriate method to handle the building blocks of big data.
2. Apply fundamental techniques like Hadoop / Map Reduce in solving real world Big Data
problems.
3. Analyze big data analytics in various applications like recommender systems, Social
Networks & Streams.
4. Develop Appropriate Cloud computing solutions & deployment of service models.
